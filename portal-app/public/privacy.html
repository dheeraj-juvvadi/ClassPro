Privacy & storage.

Anon brings your SRM attendance, marks and timetable into one place. This page describes the data used by the current service.

Remembered sessions

If you choose “Keep me signed in,” the server stores your session, credentials, cookies and reports in an encrypted database with a seven-day inactivity expiry. The encryption key is held on the same server. This protects database contents at rest; it does not protect against full server compromise. A session cookie in your browser identifies the server session.

Signing out deletes your active Anon session and local report cache. It does not revoke sessions directly with SRM. Deployment rollback backups may retain an earlier encrypted session snapshot; deleting the active session does not immediately erase those backups.

Storage on your device

Anon stores your latest academic reports in localStorage to show them quickly and when the network is unavailable. This includes personal and academic information and is not encrypted by Anon. Display preferences, optional schedule-period choices and your analytics choice are also stored locally. Attendance planning scenarios last for the current visit and clear on sign-out. Avoid leaving Anon signed in on a shared device.

A service worker also saves the app’s public pages, fonts, artwork and scripts so it can reopen offline after an online visit. This app-shell cache does not contain passwords, provider cookies or API responses. Your browser may clear it when storage is needed.

Optional analytics and notifications

To diagnose failures, Anon sends limited operational events to its server: error categories, affected app sections, fixed API route names, status codes, timing, script locations and release identifiers. These records exclude error-message text, stack traces, form values, cookies and academic reports. Files are kept for up to seven UTC calendar days and capped at 2 MB per day. They may be dropped when rate or storage limits are reached.

If you allow analytics, the same bounded log can also count app-section visits and time spent actively viewing each section, without an account identifier. A short-lived activity count also measures signed-in sessions active within the last two minutes. It uses your existing session in server memory; only aggregate counts are saved. Operational error reporting works independently of optional usage analytics.

You can also send an issue report using fixed categories. This saves the issue category, app section, screen-size category and app version in the same bounded log. The form does not collect free text or contact details. A successful confirmation means it was saved; it does not promise a response time.

With your permission, Vercel Analytics and Speed Insights measure visits and page performance. Anon does not intentionally send passwords, provider cookies or report contents to analytics. Optional analytics scripts are not loaded until you allow them. Hosting and security request logs can still be generated when the service is used.

Browser notifications require a separate permission. Class reminders may display course names and rooms on your device. They run while Anon is open; they are not background push notifications.

Service providers and your choices

The frontend is hosted by Vercel; the portal service runs on Microsoft Azure. SRM processes the portal requests sent to its systems. You can sign out, clear this site’s browser data, disable notifications in browser settings, and decline optional analytics.

Use the in-app problem report for service issues. Do not share passwords, cookies or private academic records.
