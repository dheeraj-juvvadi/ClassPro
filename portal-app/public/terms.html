Terms of use.

Anon is a student-built interface for viewing SRM portal information. It is not an official SRM service.

Use your own accounts

Connect only accounts you are authorized to access. Keep your passwords private and sign out on shared devices. Do not use Anon to access another student’s records, bypass access controls or overload Anon or SRM services.

Your academic records

SRM’s official records remain the source for academic decisions. Anon can display cached, incomplete or delayed information when a portal is unavailable. Check the displayed sync time and confirm important information with the official portal or your institution.

Attendance calculations and leave projections are estimates based on available hours, timetables and day orders. They do not submit leave requests, change attendance records or guarantee eligibility. Calendar changes and institutional rules may affect the result.

Availability and connections

Access depends on SRM’s systems, your credentials, network conditions and Anon’s capacity. Connections may expire or require verification. Features and availability can change as the service is maintained.

Data and preferences

See Privacy & storage for how credentials, remembered sessions, browser reports, analytics and notifications work. You can stop using the service and sign out at any time.

Questions and problem reports

Report a problem using the in-app form. Do not share passwords, cookies or private academic records.
